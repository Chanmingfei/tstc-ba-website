Freshman Guide (10): Library & Study Spaces

This guide covers everything you need to know about using the library and finding study spaces on campus. We will start with the library, then explain how to use empty classrooms and lecture halls, and finish with tips on choosing the right place for different study needs.

I. Library Guide

1. Floor guide

The library has a new building and an old building. Main services are concentrated on floors 2 to 5. After entering through the main entrance, use the floor guide below to find what you need:

  • Floor 2 (main entrance): First and Second Book Lending Areas, Service Desk, Reading Pavilion, Smart Piano, Red Literature Reading Zone, Party Newspaper Reading Zone, Free Reading Area;
  • Floor 3: Third and Fourth Book Lending Areas, Student Study Room;
  • Floor 4: First and Second Sample Reading Rooms, Newspaper Archive, Academic Lecture Hall, Offices (Circulation & Reading Department, Special Collections Department, Readers Association Room);
  • Floor 5: Periodical Reading Room, Cloud Desktop Reading Room, Electronic Reading Room, Audio-Visual Materials Library, Quiet Reading Zone (where quiet reading aloud is allowed);
  • Dense Stack: Located on Floor 1 of the old building. Readers cannot enter directly; make an appointment at the Floor 2 Service Desk.

2. Opening hours

Except for the Electronic Reading Room and a few special zones, daily opening hours are consistent. Winter/summer vacations and holidays may have separate arrangements; check the library's official notices for updates.

Area Hours Notes
Floors 2–5 of new and old buildings Daily 7:00–22:00 Open on Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays
Electronic Reading Room (old building, Floor 5) Summer: 8:00–11:45, 14:30–18:00
Winter: 8:00–11:45, 14:00–17:30		 Open Monday–Friday only; closed Friday afternoon, weekends, and holidays
Digital resources 24/7 all year Available during vacations

Note: when the Electronic Reading Room is closed, the ordinary reading and study areas on Floors 2–5 stay open. Do not assume the whole library is closed.

3. Entry methods

The library turnstiles support several verification methods. Entry is for the cardholder only; tailgating is not allowed. Please note: NFC, face recognition, and QR codes are for entry only. To borrow books you must bring your physical campus card.

  • Campus card: Tap your physical card on the turnstile reader;
  • Face recognition: Look at the turnstile camera to enter;
  • Phone NFC: Add your campus card to your phone wallet, then tap your phone on the NFC reader;
  • Perfect Campus app QR code: Open the app, tap “Pay” on the home page to show the QR code, and scan it at the turnstile. No charge is made.

Important: Phone NFC, face recognition, and the Perfect Campus QR code only allow you to enter the library for reading. They cannot be used to borrow books. Always bring your physical campus card when you want to check out books.

4. Seat booking system

Library seats must be reserved through a WeChat public account. Follow “唐山师范学院图书馆”, tap “微服务” → “座位预约”; you can also use the “我去图书馆” account. On first login, bind your phone number, set a password, and select “唐山师范学院主校区”.

The basic flow is: reserve online → check in on time → manually release the seat when finished. If you need to step away temporarily, use “暂离” (temporary leave) to hold your seat. You can check in by scanning a QR code, tapping the check-in screen on the Floor 2 seat-selection kiosk, or using Bluetooth.

Booking time rules

  • System open: 4:30–22:00 daily; no new bookings in the last 10 minutes before closing;
  • Early booking (4:30–7:00): seat held until 7:30;
  • Real-time booking (7:00–21:50): check in within 30 minutes after selecting a seat;
  • Next-day booking: open 14:00–22:00 daily; check in before 7:30 the next day.

Temporary leave and release rules

  • 30-minute leave: for errands or looking up materials;
  • 60-minute leave: for lunch/dinner (available 11:00–13:00 and 17:00–19:00 only);
  • Maximum 3 leaves within any 2-hour window; leave status ends automatically when time expires;
  • You must manually release your seat when done; after releasing, you must wait 1 minute before booking again.

Seat occupation reporting: If a seat is booked but unoccupied or occupied only by belongings, you can report it by uploading a photo. The reported user has 15 minutes to check in again; otherwise they receive 1 violation and the seat is released to you. Seats in “reserved” or “temporarily away” status cannot be reported; lounge chairs and sofas are not in the booking system. You can report up to 5 times per day; malicious reporting will get you blacklisted, and each successful report earns 15 points.

Violations and blacklist: 3 violations within 15 days (failure to check in, failure to release seat, or failure to re-check-in after a report) will automatically place you on the blacklist for 7 days. You earn points for daily check-in, temporary leaves, and reports, up to 40 points per day. Items in the points shop have different uses; an item that clears violation records does not remove a blacklist.

Lounge chairs and sofas in the connecting corridor are not in the booking system and do not require a reservation. If the network is slow, you can also use the Floor 2 seat-selection kiosks to book or check in.

5. Quiet and reading-aloud zones

Reading areas must be kept quiet; do not read aloud or talk loudly. If you need to recite or read aloud, go to the Quiet Reading Zone on Floor 5. Only plain water or bottled water is allowed inside; meals, milk tea, and strong-smelling snacks are prohibited.

6. Borrowing password

The initial borrowing password for new students is the last six digits of your ID card number. If the last digit is the letter X, change the password as soon as possible. You can change it in two ways:

  • Go to the Service Desk on Floor 2 and ask staff to help;
  • Log in to the MiaoSi library system, then click “密码修改” on the left side.

7. Borrowing and returning books

Before borrowing, check the library catalog for the book's location and call number. You can search via the library WeChat account (“我的图书馆” → “馆藏查询”) or the “馆藏 OPAC” link on the library website.

How to borrow and return:

  1. Find the book using its call number in the correct lending area;
  2. Borrow it at the self-service machine near that lending area with your campus card and the book;
  3. Return it on time to the same lending area where you borrowed it. Returns do not require a campus card, and you can ask someone else to return books for you;
  4. Dense-stack books: If the catalog location is “密集书库”, register at the Floor 2 Service Desk. Books are retrieved every Tuesday and Thursday afternoon.

Keep the due date in mind. Lost or damaged books are handled according to the library's official compensation rules.

8. Internet and help

The library provides free WiFi. While on campus you can access CNKI and other academic databases directly. For off-campus access, follow the remote access guide on the library website. If you have problems with seat booking, borrowing, passwords, or facilities, go to the Floor 2 Service Desk during staffed hours.

II. Using Empty Classrooms and Lecture Halls

Any teaching building or lecture hall that has no scheduled class and is powered can be used for self-study, with no restriction by grade or department. Please follow these rules:

  1. Keep quiet: No chatting or phone speakers. Set your phone to silent or vibrate, and step outside to take calls;
  2. Classes come first: Teaching activities have priority. If a teacher or class needs the room, pack up and leave promptly;
  3. Keep it clean: Take your trash with you, do not draw on desks, do not move furniture unnecessarily, and turn off lights and fans when you leave;
  4. Watch your belongings: Classrooms are unattended. Keep valuables with you, and do not leave books or items to reserve seats for long periods.

III. Choosing a Study Space

The library and empty classrooms each suit different situations:

  • Short homework sessions, flexible schedules, free coming and going → choose empty classrooms or lecture halls;
  • Long-term exam prep, reading professional books, needing a fixed quiet seat, or reading aloud → choose the library.
